## Ideal Customer Profile ✅

- **Size:** mid-market, ~50–1,000 employees (densest 50–200). Tipalti brands this the "Mighty Mid-Market." Contrary states the ICP as "mid-market companies with 50–1,000 employees using ERP systems."
- **Revenue band:** ~$10M–$1B, densest at $10M–$50M; mid-market reportedly ~75% of revenue. 🟡 (third-party aggregator).
- **Fit triggers:** scaling/high-growth and wants to **add AP volume without adding finance headcount**; runs a real ERP (NetSuite/OneWorld, Intacct, QuickBooks); has **international payees + tax-compliance pain.** ✅
- **Economic buyer:** CFO / Controller / AP Manager (often CPA/MBA, 35–55). ✅
- **NOT a fit:** tiny SMBs with simple, mostly-domestic AP and a 1–2 person finance team → that's **Bill.com** territory. Tipalti is "overkill / overcomplicated" for straightforward domestic AP, and pricier/slower to implement. ✅

## Verticals — and the unifying logic ✅

The thread across every strong vertical: **they pay large numbers of distributed, often international payees** (sellers, creators, affiliates, publishers, developers, players). That's the mass-payments sweet spot — onboarding + tax forms + FX + 50+ methods to 200+ countries + OFAC.

Rough mix (~2025, aggregator) 🟡: SaaS/tech ~30%, creator economy + marketplaces ~20%, gaming/e-sports ~15%; expanding into healthcare + manufacturing since 2024 to de-cyclicalize.

Strong verticals with dedicated pages/case studies ✅:
- **Digital advertising / ad-tech** (publisher payouts) — GumGum, PubMatic, Centerfield, Freestar, Sovrn, Aragon Advertising.
- **Affiliate / performance marketing** — integrates with Everflow, Phonexa, Swaarm.
- **Creator economy / influencer payouts.**
- **Online marketplaces / e-commerce.**
- **Gaming / e-sports** — prize + player/creator payouts.
- **SaaS / software** — Stack Overflow, Lucidworks, Amplitude.
- **Media / streaming** — TuneIn, DLive, Vimeo, National Geographic.

## The four distinct use cases / personas ✅

1. **Marketplace / platform mass-payouts** — pay sellers, creators, drivers, hosts, players, affiliates, publishers. Self-service onboarding + tax + FX to 200+ countries. (Roblox, Twitch, Uber, Skillshare, Challengermode, Gen.G.)
2. **High-growth SaaS scaling AP without scaling finance** — "avoid hires, accelerate close." (Stack Overflow, Lucidworks.)
3. **Multi-entity / multi-subsidiary global AP on NetSuite OneWorld** — PO + 2-/3-way matching, subsidiary-level sync, one centralized Multi-FX account vs a regional bank network. (Cited ed-tech firm runs 8 subsidiaries in one place, 25%+ faster close.)
4. **Paying international contractors/suppliers across many countries** — global tax compliance (W-9/W-8/1099/1042-S), OFAC, 120 currencies. (Therabody, ImaginAb, ManyPets.)

## Day-to-day users ✅

- **AP clerk / AP manager** — biggest workload-reduction beneficiary.
- **Controller** — close, reconciliation, ERP sync, audit.
- **CFO** — economic buyer.
- **Payee/supplier** — Supplier Hub self-service: own onboarding, payment status/history, detail updates without AP involvement (27+ languages).

## Geography ✅

US-heavy but increasingly global. HQ Foster City; biggest offices Tel Aviv, SF, Vancouver + London/Toronto/Amsterdam/Tbilisi. Customer base "primarily US-based," European expansion from 2023 (London + Netherlands, targeting Germany/Nordics). Customers themselves are often multi-region by nature. Payment **reach** is global regardless of customer HQ.

## Who it does NOT fit ✅

Retention is high (~99% claimed) but review themes are consistent on misfit:
- **Overkill for simpler/smaller/domestic AP** — clearest non-fit signal.
- **Steep learning curve / non-intuitive UI**, long implementation ("months regardless of size").
- **Limited reporting / no AP-leadership dashboards.**
- **Payment-execution complaints** (delays, fees) and **pre-funding drag.**
- **AR-focused needs** — Tipalti is AP/payouts-centric; Bill.com does both AP and AR.

Net non-fit: very small businesses, domestic-only/low-complexity AP, teams wanting plug-and-play onboarding, and AR-led buyers.
