## Why these three are here

Decimal is stablecoin-native AP on Solana. AP eventually bumps into **contractor payouts + tax compliance** — exactly what these three do. They're payroll/payout-adjacent to AP: useful as **table-stakes blueprints and partner candidates**, mostly not direct competitors.

## The three, briefly

**Toku** — crypto-native, compliance-first **token payroll + EOR** for token-paying companies/DAOs (Filecoin Foundation, Protocol Labs, Gitcoin, Safe). Founding wedge = **token-compensation tax** (409A token options, 83(b), USD-equivalent withholding) — the deepest of any player here. 2025–26 it added stablecoin payroll rails via Mesh/Rain, launched on Polygon + Sei, integrates ADP/Workday. ~$20M raised, Blockchain Capital-led. ✅

**Rise** — global **hybrid payroll**: fund in fiat or USDC, workers withdraw in fiat/stablecoin/crypto, routed via proprietary smart contracts (ETH/Arbitrum/OP/Polygon/Avalanche). Circle (USDC) partner, FinCEN MSB, SOC 2, EOR + AOR, W-8/W-9 + 1099. >$1B total payroll, 190+ countries. ~$10M raised. The most "self-custodial-flavored" architecture of the three. ✅

**Trolley** (ex-Payment Rails) — the **TradFi outlier**: mass payouts (ACH/SEPA/bank/PayPal/card, Thunes for instant) for marketplaces/creators/gig (Envato, Canva, Epic, SoundCloud). **No crypto rail.** But the **deepest payee tax-reporting engine** — W-9/W-8, 1099 & 1042-S, first end-to-end **DAC7** product, OECD reporting. >1.1M recipients, 210+ countries. ~$38M raised. ✅

## Comparison verdicts

- **Most crypto-native:** Toku (born for token comp); Rise close second (smart-contract payout engine, Circle partner).
- **Most TradFi:** Trolley (no crypto rail, best tax reporting).
- **Best token-comp tax:** Toku, decisively. **Best payee info-reporting (1099/1042-S/DAC7):** Trolley, decisively. **EOR across many countries:** Toku & Rise.
- **None is Solana-native** — Toku on Polygon/Sei, Rise on ETH/L2s, Trolley on no chain.

## Relevance to Decimal

- **Trolley = the model to copy + a partner candidate, not a competitor.** Its W-8/W-9 → 1099/1042-S → DAC7 stack is the AP-side tax table-stakes blueprint. No crypto rail / no Solana → doesn't compete for stablecoin-native demand. Decimal could white-label/partner its tax engine rather than rebuild.
- **Rise = the nearest competitor in spirit** (stablecoin funding, smart-contract payouts, contractor invoices, W-8/W-9/1099 + EOR) — but payroll-positioned and not on Solana. Direct head-to-head only if Decimal drifts into contractor/payroll. Also a payroll-layer partner candidate.
- **Toku = partner/precedent for the hardest compliance** (token comp, EOR) Decimal should NOT build in-house. Its strategy — *be the compliance brain on top of stablecoin rails, not just another rail* — is the single most instructive precedent for Decimal.

**Bottom line:** copy Trolley's tax-reporting completeness (the real AP table stakes), watch Rise as the nearest stablecoin-payout competitor / possible partner, treat Toku as the compliance partner+precedent. **The white space all three confirm: none is Solana-native** — an open rail position for a Solana-native stablecoin AP product that answers the 1099/1042-S/W-8/W-9 bar Trolley has already set.
