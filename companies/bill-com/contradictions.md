## Patterns observed

1. **BILL's headline numbers (480K customers, 250M invoices, 80% reduction) all dance on the edge of marketing.** They are roughly true but use definitions favorable to BILL. No outright lies, but they would not survive a buyer's deep diligence question.

2. **The 4-SKU marketing surface understates the real product footprint.** BILL has 9 buyable SKUs as of June 2026. A competitor benchmarking against the 4 will miss Procurement, 1099, API Platform, Insights, Connect.

3. **Float-income disagreement between streams suggests the FY25 10-K should be the single source of truth for any forward modeling.** The $148M-$161.8M-$167M range across docs reflects FY periods, segment definitions, and partial-year reporting — be careful which number you cite.

4. **Bank partner confusion is real.** BILL deliberately does not publish a definitive FBO custodian list; Cross River is the card-issuer, not necessarily the FBO custodian; JPM Chase is investor + distribution partner but also likely a custodian. A competitor wanting to attack the "concentration risk" angle should ask BILL directly in any sales / partnership conversation.

5. **The "94% NRR" number is hidden in plain sight.** It's in the 10-K but absent from BILL's marketing — the single most strategically damning data point in the file.

6. **The "Augusta" acquisition was never real.** Confirmed acquisition list: Divvy (May 2021), Invoice2go (Sept 2021), Finmark (Nov 2022). Three, not four.

7. **The Lacerte family lineage is real but the specific genealogical link to Larry Lacerte (Lacerte Software / Intuit 1998) is undocumented in the public sources we searched.** Treat it as plausible-but-unverified.
