## Ideal Customer Profile ✅

**Crypto-native organizations that pay many people/vendors in tokens & stablecoins and want approvals + audit trail + accounting export instead of raw multisig transactions.** Buyer = the crypto-org finance lead / "Web3 CFO" / treasury or ops manager (not a developer). Request publishes a "Web3 CFO guide" — that's who they sell to.

Segments by fit:
1. **DAOs & Web3 protocols** paying contributors, core teams, grantees — the original wedge. Flow: contributor invoices → finance approves → pays from a Safe multisig → Request keeps the books.
2. **Foundations / treasuries** managing payouts via Safe (Request is embedded in the Safe Apps interface).
3. **Crypto startups** running USDC/USDT payroll & contractor payments.
4. **Freelancers / contractors** invoicing crypto clients (the AR side — also the top-of-funnel that pulls in paying companies).
5. **Increasingly: traditional/professional-services firms** dabbling in crypto (Deloitte, PwC logos) — signals an upmarket / fiat+crypto-hybrid push. 🟡

Skews small-to-mid crypto-native teams; ~2,300+ businesses self-reported.

## Distinct use cases ✅

1. **DAO/protocol pays contributors & grantees in stablecoins** — core wedge (invoice → approve → pay from Safe).
2. **Crypto startup runs payroll in USDC** — supported, but **contractor-style payouts + recordkeeping, NOT Employer-of-Record/tax-withholding** (that's Toku/Rise/Deel territory).
3. **Freelancer invoices crypto clients** — AR side; doubles as customer-acquisition funnel.
4. **Foundation manages treasury payouts via Safe multisig** — native Safe integration, batch payments.

## Day-to-day users

- **Crypto-org finance/treasury lead** — economic buyer + heaviest user (creates/approves/pays, reconciles).
- **Multisig signers** — co-sign payment batches in Request or directly in Safe.
- **Contributor/contractor** — submits invoices, gets paid to their own wallet.

## Geography

EU-headquartered (Paris), global remote customer base concentrated in the crypto-native world (DAOs/protocols are jurisdictionally distributed). Off-ramp now reaches 190+ countries / 15+ fiat currencies.

## Relevance to Decimal

Request's customer base **is the crypto-native AP demand Decimal is chasing** — DAOs, protocols, foundations, crypto startups paying contributors from a multisig. The critical observation: **Request serves this demand on EVM + Safe; Decimal serves it on Solana + Squads.** For a Solana-native team or protocol, Decimal is the more natural fit, and Request's own data (Solana ~6% of its volume) shows it under-serves that segment. The risk: Request's customers are *also* heavily Safe/Gnosis users on EVM, and crypto-native teams increasingly hold treasury across chains — so Decimal's wedge is strongest with **Solana-first orgs** (the Solana DeFi/infra ecosystem) rather than chain-agnostic DAOs already standardized on Safe + Request.
