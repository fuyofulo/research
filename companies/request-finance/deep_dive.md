## TL;DR

Request Finance is **the crypto-native "Bill.com"** — a non-custodial AP / AR / invoicing / payroll / expenses / accounting SaaS for crypto-native orgs (DAOs, protocols, foundations, Web3 startups, and the contractors they pay), built on top of the open-source **Request Network** protocol. ✅ It is **invoicing-led and EVM-first** (Solana is listed but ~6% of volume). It is the clear leader of the *surviving* crypto-native AP/invoicing category — but it's a **modest, cyclical business**: **~$1.3B cumulative payment volume all-time (Jan 2026)**, ~$24–34M/month, 2,300+ businesses. ✅

The honest one-liner: *a genuine category leader in crypto-native AP/invoicing with marquee logos and ~$1.3B lifetime volume — but a small business in a small, cyclical TAM, whose headline marketing ("$2B," "43% of teams," "Bill.com for crypto") is inflated or misattributed, whose REQ token is economically irrelevant, and which is squeezed from both sides (TradFi adding stablecoins; Solana-native + platform-owners consolidating). Its survival play — acquiring its way into fiat rails + accounting and courting MiCA/Bpifrance — is the right move precisely because the pure crypto-native niche is no longer enough.*

## 1. What it is + the protocol/company split

- **Request Network** = the open-source, decentralized protocol for on-chain payment requests (smart contracts + SDK, Ethereum-anchored), run by the **Request Network Foundation**, governed by the **REQ token**. Brands itself "Get Paid & Pay In Crypto. No Custody." ✅
- **Request Finance** = the for-profit company/app built on that protocol — the polished B2B product. "Spun off from Request Network as two separate teams." ✅

The two are now distinct entities. REQ accrues to the protocol/Foundation and is largely **decoupled** from Request Finance's SaaS revenue. ✅

## 2. Founders & history

- **Founders:** Christophe Lassuyt (CEO) + Etienne Tatur (CTO). ✅ Previously co-founded **Moneytis** (2015, cross-border remittance comparison, YC 2017, shut down Nov 2019 to pivot to blockchain). Tatur was ex-lead-dev at Qobuz. ✅
- **Timeline:** 2017 Request Network founded (Paris) + REQ ICO → V1 Mar 2018 → "Request Invoicing" Jul 2020 → **Request Finance app launched Jan 2021** (started at ~1 user / 115 tx). Company dated founded 2020, Paris-based, remote team across ~12 countries. ✅

## 3. Funding

- **2017 Request Network ICO:** ~**$33.6M** (seed + public sale; 500M REQ at $0.0672, sold out in ~3 days, accepted ETH). Investors incl. **Y Combinator**, Plasma Capital. ✅
- **June 2022 — $5.5M seed (Request Finance the company):** led by **Balderton Capital, XAnge, Animoca Brands**; angels **Sebastien Borget** (Sandbox), **Michael Kong** (Fantom), **Stani Kulechov** (Aave). ✅
- **March 2025 — strategic round (undisclosed amount):** led by **Bpifrance** (French sovereign bank), w/ Balderton, XAnge + key clients. Announced alongside the $1B-payments milestone; framed as fuel for stablecoin/fiat scale + MiCA compliance. ✅
- **Total raised:** ~$33.6M (2017 protocol ICO) + $5.5M (2022 equity) + undisclosed 2025 strategic. No clean consolidated total (separate entities/instruments). 🟡

## 4. Traction (2025–26)

- **Cumulative volume:** $1B (Mar 2025) → $1.2B+ (Oct/Nov 2025) → **$1.3B+ all-time (Jan 2026).** ✅
- **Monthly run-rate:** ~$24–34M/month, ~4,000–5,600 payments/month (Oct 2025: $33.6M; Jan 2026: $23.8M). Implies ~$300M+/yr. ✅
- **Stablecoin share:** ~88–90%+ of payments; USDC the single most-used currency (~43% of payments). ✅
- **Customers:** **2,300+ businesses.** ✅/🔴
- **Named customers:** Aave, The Sandbox, The Graph, MakerDAO/Sky, OpenZeppelin, Arbitrum, Ledger, Decentraland, Bonk, Beam, Gnosis/Safe; pro-services names Deloitte, PwC, Balderton, XAnge. ✅ (Caveat: several marquee logos overlap with seed investors — discount the logo wall.)

**⚠️ Corrections to common claims:**
- **NOT "$2B/yr."** Real figure is ~$1.3B cumulative all-time (Jan 2026). The "$300M" sometimes cited is stale (2022–23 level) or a differently-scoped subset.
- **"43% of crypto finance teams use it"** is unverified — the real 43% is **USDC's share of payments**, a currency-mix stat misread as market share. 🔴

## 5. The REQ token

Price ~$0.05–0.06, market cap ~$40–49M (June 2026), down ~95% from the Jan-2018 ATH of $1.06. 1B max supply. **Users do NOT need REQ** to use the app or pay. On-chain utility is a tiny anti-spam burn (~582K REQ burned cumulatively = ~0.073% of supply — economically trivial). Trades on speculation, decoupled from the SaaS business. **The token is a distraction/overhang, not a value-accrual engine.** ✅

## 6. Custody / regulatory posture

- **Non-custodial by design for the crypto spine.** Request Network: "We never hold the money… straight to your wallet." The app generates payment requests; **the user signs from their own wallet/Safe multisig**; funds move wallet-to-wallet. This is the architectural heart. ✅
- **The hybrid caveat (2024–26):** Request bolted **custodial fiat rails** on top via acquisitions — **Pay.so → "Request Technologies"** (crypto↔fiat off-ramp) and a new **"Global USD Account"** + corporate cards. These *do* touch money through regulated partners. So "touches no money, unlike Bill.com/Tipalti" is true for the crypto AP/AR/payroll spine but **no longer universally true.** 🟡
- **Regulatory:** EU/French company. Off-ramp runs through **Request Technologies UAB**, a registered **VASP in Lithuania**; KYB required for fiat. **Actively pursuing MiCA** compliance. No US MTL posture advertised (the non-custody model sidesteps it on the crypto leg). ✅

## 7. 2025–26 news

- **March 2024 — acquired Consola Finance** (Vienna crypto-accounting fintech) → now "Request Accounting." ✅
- **Acquired Pay.so** → "Request Technologies" (crypto↔fiat on/off-ramp). ✅
- **March 2025 — $1B milestone + Bpifrance-led strategic round.** ✅
- **2025–26 direction:** heavy push toward **stablecoin + fiat "CFO stack,"** Global USD Account, off-ramp to 190+ countries / 15+ fiat currencies, Aleo private payroll, MiCA-readiness. ✅

## Bottom line for Decimal

**This is the most important competitor we hadn't researched — and the most nuanced.** Three takeaways:

1. **Request is Decimal's true workflow-twin on the AP/invoicing axis** (Altitude is the *account* twin; Request is the *workflow* twin). It already owns the crypto-native finance lead, the marquee DAO/protocol logos, and the "invoice → approve → pay from multisig → keep the books" flow Decimal is building.

2. **But its center of gravity is EVM, not Solana.** Solana is ~6% of its volume and not in its developer API. That is **Decimal's single clearest opening** — a genuinely Solana-native AP product fills a rail position Request only peripherally serves, and that no survivor (Sphere is Solana-first but payments-API-led, not AP-workflow-led) fully owns.

3. **Request's evolution is Decimal's roadmap preview.** Request started non-custodial-crypto-only and is *acquiring its way into fiat off-ramp + accounting + MiCA* because the pure crypto-native TAM is too small and cyclical. Decimal should expect the same gravitational pull — and the same table-stakes gaps Request is racing to close (native fiat off-ramp, deep accounting sync, and US tax forms W-8/W-9/1099, which Request's own product appears weaker on than Tipalti/Trolley).
