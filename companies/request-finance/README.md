## Headline finding

Request Finance is **Decimal's true workflow-twin** (Altitude is the account-twin). A non-custodial AP/AR/invoicing/payroll/expenses/accounting SaaS for crypto-native orgs (DAOs, protocols, foundations, Web3 startups), built on the open-source **Request Network** protocol. It leads the *surviving* crypto-native AP/invoicing category — but it's a **modest, cyclical business** (~$1.3B cumulative volume all-time as of Jan 2026, ~$24–34M/month, 2,300+ businesses) and **EVM-first** (Solana ~6% of volume, not in its API).

The honest one-liner: *the genuine category leader in crypto-native AP/invoicing, with marquee logos and real product — but a small business in a small TAM, with inflated headline stats, an irrelevant token, and a two-sided squeeze, now buying its way (Consola accounting, Pay.so fiat off-ramp, MiCA, Bpifrance) toward a fiat+crypto CFO stack because the pure crypto-native niche isn't enough.*

**Why it matters for Decimal:** Request proves the non-custodial, multisig-gated AP model works at $1B+ scale — and previews Decimal's roadmap pressures. **Decimal's cleanest wedge is Solana-native depth** (the rail Request under-serves). The defensible position is the intersection: *better than Altitude on AP-workflow depth, more Solana-native than Request.*
