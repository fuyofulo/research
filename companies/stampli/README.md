# Stampli (stampli.com) — Research Folder

Compiled 2026-06-06. Researched as the **AI-native, experience-leading** mid-market AP player (the one that consistently beats Tipalti/BILL on UX & implementation speed).

Confidence: ✅ high · 🟡 medium · 🔴 low/incorrect-premise.

---

## Headline finding

Stampli is an **AI-native, collaboration-first AP (now procure-to-pay) platform** that centers the entire invoice lifecycle — capture, coding, approvals, communication, payment — *on the invoice itself*, with an embedded AI assistant **"Billy the Bot"** (live since ~2018, one of the earliest AP AI assistants), deployable in **~4–6 weeks on top of your existing ERP** (no migration). ✅

The honest one-liner: *the AP tool finance teams actually like to use — collaboration-first, fast to stand up on your existing ERP, with a real (not bolted-on) AP-specific AI — but it deliberately under-monetizes payments, keeps pricing opaque, and now has to prove its decade-old "Billy" edge still matters when every rival ships "agentic AI."*

## Fundamentals (premise correction)

- Founders **Eyal Feldman (CEO) + Ofer Feldman (CTO)** — **brothers** (NOT "Roi Kliper" 🔴). Founded **2015**, HQ Mountain View CA + Israel R&D. ✅
- **Funding:** Seed ~$3M → A $6.7M (2018, SignalFire) → B $25M (2019, SignalFire) → C $50M (May 2021, Insight) → **D $61M (Oct 2023, Blackstone-led)**. **Total ~$145–150M; valuation undisclosed.** No new raise found through mid-2026. ✅

## Product & AI

- **Invoice-centric collaboration hub** (all comms/approvals on the invoice) — the differentiating design choice. + **Billy the Bot** (ML auto-coding, routing, duplicate/fraud detection, learns each customer's GL patterns) + **Stampli Card** (corporate cards, ~1% cashback) + **Direct Pay** (ACH/check/virtual card/international) + **Procure-to-Pay** (GA Q1 2025, moving upstream into POs). ✅
- **AI story:** Billy since ~2018 → reframed 2025 as **"agentic AI / AI employee"** claiming it performs ~86% of finance work across 2,500+ fields, trained on 83M+ hours of finance work (vendor-reported, unaudited 🟡). **Honest read: a genuine pioneer/leader on practical embedded AP-specific AI, but only matching — not clearly leading — on the buzzier agentic-copilot framing where Ramp markets harder.** Depth/proof over flashy breadth.

## Money model — the notable differentiator

CEO: *"we consider payments to be a commodity, not a revenue generator."* **No pre-funding/float** required for batch ACH; bank statements show each transaction individually (not lump sums) — strongly implying **NOT a classic FBO/float model**. Charges minimal payment fees (a $5K ACH "<$1" vs ~$175 via virtual-card models). **Primary monetization = SaaS subscription** (volume + users + integrations, custom-quoted); payments/interchange are light, secondary. Sponsor bank/ODFI not disclosed. ✅/🟡

## ERP, customers, competitive

- **70+ ERP integrations** (NetSuite, Sage Intacct, QuickBooks, MS Dynamics/BC/F&O, Sage 100/300, Acumatica, Oracle, SAP) — "keep your ERP, deploy in weeks" (~4–6 wks vs Tipalti's 6–18 months). ✅
- **~1,600 customers, $80B+ annual invoice value** (2024). Named: Purple, Techstars, Cork Howard Construction. Mid-market across manufacturing/construction/healthcare/hospitality. **G2 ~4.6/5, repeatedly #1 mid-market for Ease of Setup & Quality of Support.** ✅
- **Position:** between BILL (SMB, transparent pricing, rule-based) and Tipalti (enterprise global, slow implementation), with Ramp/Airbase pressing from the card/spend angle. Moat = invoice-centric UX + early/deep AI + broad/fast ERP integration. **No AR module.** ✅

## Relevance to Decimal

Stampli matters to Decimal for **two reasons beyond competition**: (1) **The "payments-as-commodity, no float" stance validates Decimal's thesis from the TradFi side** — a respected AP leader deliberately refuses the float/interchange game and monetizes software instead, exactly the model self-custodial USDC enables. (2) **Stampli is the UX/implementation bar.** Its dominance on ease-of-use and 4–6-week deployment is *the* reason mid-market buyers pick it over Tipalti/BILL — a reminder that in AP, **time-to-value and collaboration UX win deals, not rails.** Decimal's on-chain settlement is meaningless if the product isn't as pleasant and fast-to-deploy as Stampli. Not a direct competitor (US, fiat, no crypto), but the clearest lesson in *what actually wins mid-market AP*.
